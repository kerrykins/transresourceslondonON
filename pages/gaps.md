Each and every one of our experiences during our journey is uniquely beautiful. As a transfemimine non-binary person, I acknowledge that there are considerable information gaps given my own personal lived experiences. For instance, I am more knowledgeable about transfeminine hormone therapy than transmasculine hormone therapy. 

Because of this, I have made efforts to research and provide resources on topics that I have no lived experience. Despite that, I am aware there remains some information gaps. 

Please [message me on Discord](https://discord.com/users/292823530637361154) if you feel there is something to add or adjust in the guide. I *really* welcome it. Please let me know you're DM'ing me about the guide or I might not accept your DM. 

Do note that the guide's scope, especially in the London, ON sections, is firmly focused on resources in the city. I defer to other guides for local resources in other areas of Ontario. On the other hand, if there is additional information or corrections to be added/made in the more general sections, I'd love to hear it!

~Kerry

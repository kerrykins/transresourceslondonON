## Introduction 

Waiting lists, living in remote regions, medical gatekeeping, hostile healthcare providers, and costly private healthcare are some of the reasons why trans people may turn to DIYing HRT: hormone replacement therapy without a prescription or away from a medical clinic. Simply put, some trans people experienced systemic barriers and/or want more control over their HRT regimen. 

I acknowledge that DIYing HRT has a place in the trans community. Thus, the purpose of this page is to compile a list of resources so that those accessing HRT on their own can do it more safely. It will include information for transfeminine, transmasculine, and non-binary people.

### Risks 

A common trap in DIY transition spaces is that more = faster changes. In many cases, a massive chemical input does not force a faster physical output. Moreover, this has potential to backfire and cause harm. 

### Transmasculine HRT Risks

Testosterone and anabolic steroids are often restricted (or outright banned) substances, depending on the jurisdiction. This means that buying and/or selling testosterone can carry a much higher legal risk. Familiarise yourself with the law.

## Accessing Medical Gender Affirming Care

{% include important.html content="You cannot be denied care or a referral based on a provider's personal, moral, or religious beliefs. " %}

**Gender affirming medical care** (GAC) includes a range of treatments such as hormone therapy, voice training, surgeries, and permanent hair removal. However, this part will focus specifically on hormone replacement therapy (HRT) and OHIP-funded surgeries. 

Accessing medical gender affirming health care (GAC) usually begins with your primary care provider (PCP). 

If your PCP feels managing GAC is beyond their clinical competency, **they have a professional obligation to provide a timely and effective referral to a qualified medical professional.** 

## Accessing Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT)

In Ontario, prescrbing hormone repalcement therapy (HRT) operates n an informed consent model. A prescriber does not need a psychological evaluation or a diagnosis of gender dysphoria to begin the process. 

A willing physician (family doctors or specialists) or a nurse practitioner can prescribe hormones once they make sure you fully understand the  medical effects, risks, and timelines. Once again, if your PCP cannot prescribe HRT, they must refer you to a specialist (like an endocrinologist) or an affirming clinic/clinician that uses informed consent. 

## Accessing Transition-Related Surgeries (TRS)

Unlike HRT's informed consent model, OHIP-funded surgeries require provincial approval. Different surgeries require different formal surgical assessments (e.g., chest surgeries require one assessment whereas bottom surgeries require two). 

After your provider submits the funding application, the Ministry of Health muskert approve it before any surgery can take place. 

See [TRS Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.rainbowhealthontario.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/RHO-GAS-FAQ.pdf) for information about assessments, referrals, and OHIP coverage.
